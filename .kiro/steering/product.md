# Product Steering

**Here Lies** is a daily haunted-crypt deduction game on Reddit where every player who dies leaves a tombstone and an epitaph for everyone after them. The whole subreddit hunts the same crypt each day, and the dead light the way.

## Core Principles

1. **Community legacy:** Every death matters. Your tombstone helps future players, and your epitaph can help or deceive.
2. **Daily ritual:** One crypt per day, everyone plays the same challenge. Fresh content at midnight UTC.
3. **Pure deduction:** Every crypt is solvable by logic alone, no guessing required. This is enforced by the reference solver.
4. **Asynchronous multiplayer:** You play solo, but you see tombstones drop in realtime as others die.
5. **Accessible mystery:** Omens are always shown with both glyphs and text labels. Full keyboard support. The game teaches through contextual hints, not walls of text.

## What Makes It Reddit-y

- **Identity and reputation:** Your epitaphs earn you titles (Honest Ghost, Trickster, Poet of the Crypt).
- **Community content:** Friday crypts are player-designed, with moderator approval.
- **Shared experience:** The daily post becomes a gathering place. The Sunday Necropolis celebrates the week.
- **Opt-in social:** The share button posts your result as a comment, explicitly and manually.

## What We Avoid

- AI-generated slop: hand-crafted art direction (linocut aesthetic), hand-tuned difficulty curves.
- Reddit branding: no Snoo jokes, no karma references.
- Common clones: this is a unique resurrection of Hunt the Wumpus (1973), not a trivia quiz or platformer.
- Auto-posting on the user's behalf: every comment requires an explicit button press.

## Success Metrics (for the hackathon judges)

- **Delightful UX:** striking visual identity (carved stone and bone-white ink), intuitive controls, satisfying reveals.
- **Polish:** judges understand the game from the demo post alone; onboarding is contextual hints, not a tutorial wall.
- **Daily content:** fair, solvable, calibrated by weekday difficulty (Mon easiest, Sun hardest).
- **Reddit community layer:** tombstones, epitaphs, voting, reputation, flair, and opt-in sharing.
